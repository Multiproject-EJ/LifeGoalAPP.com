# Island 006 — Moonveil Nexus

## Goal

Build a phone-first, fully explorable, procedural 3D island whose identity is deep celestial night: fractured floating shelves, indigo architecture, cyan/violet astral energy, warm gold construction, Noctyra, and a restored Moon Gate.

## Protected rules

- 36 live tiles, canonical transforms, center, and route visibility.
- Four fixed outer landmark anchors plus center landmark.
- 390 x 844 primary composition.
- L1-L3 structural progression for every landmark.
- Explicit high/medium/low quality scaling and reduced motion.
- No gameplay state writes in the rendering layer.

## Avoid list

- tropical daylight or turquoise ocean;
- snowy ground or bright cloud kingdom;
- flat purple recolor of a prior world;
- five near-identical domed buildings;
- baked board tiles or painted UI;
- emissive bloom that destroys form and readability;
- identical distant islands;
- arena creature cadence on a non-fifth island.
