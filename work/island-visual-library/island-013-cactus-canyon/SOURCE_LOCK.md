## Terrain and depth lock

- Foreground: fractured floating mesa cliff with deep vertical red-rock strata,
  underside shadow and a few isolated spires below the rim.
- Board ground: pale orange sand/flagstone clearing inside the railway with
  sparse route-clear cacti, fences, crates and small frontier sheds.
- Near background: rail tunnel, switchbacks, watercourse/bridge hints and
  mid-size orange buttes.
- Far background: layered Monument Valley-style towers diminishing in scale and
  saturation through peach atmospheric haze.
- Horizon/sky: warm late-afternoon peach-gold sky with pale clouds and no ocean.

- sandstone: burnt orange, terracotta and ochre strata; matte/rough with dark
  cavity seams and brighter broken edges;
- ground: pale sand and sun-bleached flagstone, quiet enough for live tiles;
- timber: dark weathered brown with lighter worn edges and visible beams;
- railway: dark steel rails, timber sleepers, iron fasteners and subtle metal
  highlights;
- accents: blackened iron, warm brass/copper, amber windows and small muted blue
  details on the locomotive;
- vegetation: varied sage/cactus greens, sparse rather than lush.
