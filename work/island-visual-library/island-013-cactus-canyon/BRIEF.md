# Island 013 — Cactus Canyon brief

Working identity: a living frontier railway settlement on a floating red-rock
mesa at warm late afternoon.

Player promise: the island should read in seconds as a tiny, working western
railroad town—train circling, windmill turning, steam breathing, flags moving
and amber windows glowing against a vast canyon.

Canonical landmarks and build stories:

- Rail Nest Waterworks: L1 nest platform and tank footings; L2 working tower,
  depot and pipe; L3 restored hatchery waterworks with egg chamber and beacon.
- Windmill Ranch Yard: L1 fenced pump shed; L2 ranch house and operating wheel;
  L3 expanded workshop, full wheel, livestock yard and premium trim.
- Showdown Signal Yard: L1 signal/corral platform; L2 switch house and challenge
  floor; L3 complete signal pavilion with audience fence and brass mechanism.
- Prospector Sheriff Archive: L1 claim desk/porch; L2 sheriff office and map
  room; L3 full archive, roof sign, blue-lit records safe and survey equipment.
- Cactus Crown Union Station: central boss progresses from finished depot keep
  to civic station complex to full bell-tower union hall with flag and warm
  gabled wings.

Ambience: locomotive orbit, wheel/rod motion, steam pulses, windmill rotation,
flags, sparse tumbleweed/dust, layered heat haze and occasional distant birds.

Avoid: baked UI/route, arena gameplay, ocean, lush palms, repeated silhouettes,
toy-plastic materials, route-obscuring props and source drift.
