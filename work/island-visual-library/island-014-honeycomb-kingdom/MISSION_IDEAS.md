# Island 014 signature-mission selection

The visual-mission ideation pass deliberately favored spectacles that reuse the
real island geometry instead of covering it with a modal or detached minigame.

1. **The Great Honeyfall** — collect four Royal Nectar drops, charge four
   reservoirs, then unleash synchronized honeyfalls and a palace coronation
   beam.
2. **Queen's First Flight** — hatch a royal bee whose luminous flight traces a
   crown over all five landmarks.
3. **The Pollination Wave** — send a golden pollen wave around the entire board,
   blooming every flower in sequence.
4. **Repair the Suncomb** — rotate giant mirrored honeycomb cells to focus a
   warm beam into the palace dome.
5. **The Nectar Floodgate** — open stepped sluices that fill transparent honey
   canals and power the city.
6. **Swarm Symphony** — conduct five bee swarms into a moving crown and spiral
   formation above the island.
7. **Waxwing Rescue** — rebuild collapsing wax bridges while rescue bees carry
   glowing brood cells to safety.
8. **The Living Archive** — awaken golden story glyphs that leave the Archives
   and orbit the palace as a luminous library.
9. **Honey Moonrise** — raise a huge amber moon lens behind the palace and align
   it with the crown beacon.
10. **The Royal Bloom** — grow one enormous flower beneath the island so its
    petals unfold around the floating rock.

## Selected mission: The Great Honeyfall

This concept has the strongest visible transformation of the island itself. It
connects collection, four readable charging stages, gooey material motion,
waterfall-scale spectacle, and the palace's vertical climax without obscuring
the board. The implementation therefore uses four visible nectar pickups, four
reservoir charges, synchronized honeyfall activation, and a final coronation
beat. Save compatibility and exactly-once completion remain canonical gameplay
service responsibilities; the Three.js world only presents that state.
