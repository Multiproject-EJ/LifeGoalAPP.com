# Island 014 — Honeycomb Kingdom brief

Working identity: a radiant floating bee civilization built from honey-gold
stone, wax, amber glass and dark bronze above a deep blue cloud sea.

Player promise: the island reads immediately as an industrious, joyful hive
kingdom—bees crossing the air, honey pouring over hexagonal terraces, flowers
feeding the city and a royal domed hive palace anchoring the board.

Canonical landmarks and additive build stories:

- Queen's Nursery (Hatchery): egg cradle and wax nursery foundation → enclosed
  incubation chambers → crowned royal nursery with honey-light beacon.
- Pollinator Yard (Habit): flower court and work beds → disciplined pollinator
  hall and training apparatus → full cooperative garden pavilion.
- Nectar Trials Pavilion (Mystery): low nectar-testing court → challenge
  mechanisms and honeycomb canopy → commissioned ceremonial trial pavilion.
- Hive Archives (Wisdom): archive plinth and book cells → reading hall and
  honeycomb stacks → domed archive with illuminated codex and wisdom crown.
- Royal Honeycomb Palace (Boss): civic hive entrance and lower court → domed
  palace body and side towers → dominant crowned palace with royal bee sigil.

Construction language: waxwood ladders, hex-cell gantries, bronze hoists,
honey-amber clamps and flower-pollen material carts. Every landmark uses the
three-role robot crew and the shared finish pop: additive parts overshoot and
settle with a brief honey-gold flash and sparkle burst.

Ambience: hero bees on curved flight paths, distant worker swarms, flower
movement, viscous honey falls, pollen motes, warm lanterns and layered floating
hive islets in bright cloud depth.

Avoid: baked UI or route, an Arena gameplay conversion, generic yellow castles,
five repeated domes, flat orange plastic, excessive bloom, unsafe strobing,
route-obscuring flowers or waterfalls, and loss of the blue-sky contrast.

Delivery: `new-base`, Standard 2–5 MiB island-specific delta target.
