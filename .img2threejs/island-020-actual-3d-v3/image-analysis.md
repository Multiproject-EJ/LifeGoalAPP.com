# Island 020 actual-3D rebuild — source analysis

## Identification

The source is a portrait mobile-game environment concept showing one floating volcanic fortress island. It is an architectural/environment reconstruction target, not a single isolated object. Suitability is conditional: the visible three-quarter/front composition is strong, while rear elevations, underside continuation, interior maze routes and building backs are inferred.

## Overall form

- One broad, deep floating basalt mass with a roughly circular upper plateau and irregular fractured cliff perimeter.
- A canonical circular board route occupies the outer upper terrace; the source's approximate tiles are composition evidence only.
- A dense concentric black-stone labyrinth rises inside the route toward a tall narrow central forge keep.
- Multiple molten channels travel outward and downward through cliff mouths, creating vertically continuous lavafalls.
- Distant volcanic spires and smoke form world-space depth behind the island.

## Macro → meso → micro

Macro systems are the continuous terrain shell, cliff/underside, contained lava network, three-ring labyrinth/citadel, five landmark plots, summit launch, front extraction route and volcanic horizon. Meso systems are terraces, retaining walls, stairs, gatehouses, true arches, buttresses, bridge landings, chimneys, roofs, skiff systems and extraction cradle. Micro systems are masonry seams, cooled crust lips, rivets, chains, vents, braziers, brass caps, runes, edge wear and sparse embers.

## Spatial relationships

- The citadel foundation is embedded into the terrain, not placed on a flat disc.
- Three concentric maze-wall families step upward toward the central keep and connect through three readable gatehouse junctions.
- Lava channels are recessed/contained between basalt banks, begin at forge outlets, and continue into real cliff cuts.
- Four external landmarks are grounded beyond the protected tile corridor with true doors and service backs.
- The Iron Skiff launches from a summit davit, follows a physically open molten channel through the labyrinth, clears the front cliff, and docks inside the Expedition Ship cradle.

## Materials and light

Worked obsidian/basalt is dark, rough and chipped; black iron is satin with warm edge response; aged brass is metallic and moderately rough; magma has a yellow-white core, orange body and dark cooling margins. Lava must illuminate adjacent geometry through real scene lighting/local emissive response while leaving the stone value structure readable. No source pixels may be used as a runtime environment plate in this family.

## Identity-defining features

1. Deep floating cliff volume with multiple vertical lavafalls.
2. Dense but legible three-level concentric labyrinth.
3. Tall central Crucible Keep with true recessed windows and entry.
4. Black-stone/orange-magma/aged-brass hierarchy.
5. Real circular board integrated around—not substituted by—the fortress.
6. Functional summit-to-front escape channel and visible extraction destination.

## Uncertainty

Rear and underside construction, the exact internal topology of the maze, hidden building elevations and Expedition Ship docking mechanics are not visible in the single source. They will be authored as secondary inference, must remain structurally credible from 360°, and may not override the visible front silhouette. Exact text, HUD, counters and baked source tile symbols are excluded.
