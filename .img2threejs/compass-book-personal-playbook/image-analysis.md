# Chapter VI Personal Playbook relief — image analysis

Reference: `personal-playbook-relief-reference-final.png`

Suitability verdict: **pass for a stylized procedural relief**. The target is a generated art-direction reference rather than a photographed manufactured object. It gives a strong silhouette, countable component hierarchy, visible material families, and a plausible shallow-relief construction. Hidden backs and exact dimensions are intentionally inferred; no manufacturing-grade match is claimed.

## 1. Identification and classification

- Work type: page-bound magical rocket cockpit relief.
- Broad class: compound hard-surface mechanical/sculptural game prop.
- Primary domain: `object`.
- Confidence: `0.98`.
- Intended use: presentation-only production Three.js relief inside the Compass Book, with independent pivots/sockets for a bounded completion ceremony.

## 2. Overall form and silhouette

- One shallow rectangular page plate holds a vertically oriented central rocket and seven radial circular or arc-bounded control modules.
- The relief is approximately bilaterally balanced around the rocket, but its module emblems are intentionally asymmetric.
- The rocket is a compound cone/cylinder/lofted-fin assembly with an aspect ratio near `3.2:1` relative to its widest fin span.
- A curved Earth/orbit horizon occupies the lower quarter; a separate seven-cell progress arc sits below it.
- The component system is volumetric: rings, collars, lenses, module faces, connectors, fins, and sockets project above the page rather than reading as a flat decal.

## 3. Macro → meso → micro decomposition

Macro assemblies:

1. page-bound indigo relief plate and gilt perimeter;
2. central rocket body;
3. seven flight-system modules;
4. radial connector harness;
5. Earth/orbit horizon;
6. seven-segment launch-window arc.

Meso assemblies:

- rocket: nose cone, upper collar, main brass hull, violet crystal window/core, paired fins, lower engine collar, violet exhaust crystal;
- ignition: outer gimbal, glass dome, violet coil, brass socket;
- momentum: outer bezel, eight-point star, amber crystal core;
- minimum power: deep circular bezel, cyan half-moon cell, dark reserve hemisphere;
- warning radar: gridded circular face, radial sweep, central spindle;
- environment shield: lower circular frame, teal hex-cell shield, perimeter clamps;
- recovery route: twin looped tubes over a teal dial, central crossover and frame;
- weekly navigation: sextant arc, radial braces, teal bearing jewel;
- connector harness: seven articulated brass tubes terminating in visible sockets;
- horizon: blue limb, dark planetary disc and bounded surface mottling;
- launch arc: seven independent amber cells in one bowed brass carrier.

Micro feature groups:

- repeated bezel rivets and collar bands;
- engraved tick marks on circular modules;
- small bracket plates at connector joints;
- faceted violet and amber crystal faces;
- hammered/brushed breakup on gilt surfaces;
- leather grain and recessed page-contact shadow;
- cyan/amber emissive accents kept subordinate to the readable DOM page.

## 4. Spatial relationships and attachments

- `<rocket, embedded-in, page plate>` through a broad rear mount and lower engine socket.
- `<each system module, socketed-to, connector harness>` through one visible brass tube and collar.
- `<connector harness, attached-to, rocket hull>` through seven independent radial sockets; no tube terminates in mid-air.
- `<environment shield, below, rocket engine>` while remaining a separate module, not an eighth rocket stage.
- `<Earth horizon, behind, rocket + lower modules>` as a shallow recessed plate.
- `<launch-window arc, below, horizon>` attached to the page by two end pivots and a central bracket.
- All seven module roots require stable local pivots; rocket core, engine, fins, horizon, and progress cells require named animation anchors.

## 5. Materials and surface response

- Page plate: opaque indigo leather, dielectric, roughness approximately `0.58–0.72`, meso grain and cavity-darkened embossing.
- Brass/gilt: metallic, roughness approximately `0.24–0.42`, hammered and brushed variation, darker patina in creases.
- Enamel modules: opaque dielectric clear-coated surfaces, roughness approximately `0.18–0.34`, distinct teal/cyan/amber/violet base colors.
- Crystal cores: transparent or semi-translucent faceted dielectric with restrained transmission and emissive inner cores.
- Earth plate: satin dark blue dielectric with a separate cyan limb; it must not become a photographic texture dependency.
- Channels remain independent: albedo, roughness, bump/normal, AO, and emissive masks must not alias one another.

## 6. Color and finish

- Dominant low-value, low-saturation indigo base.
- Mid/high-value aged brass establishes all structural edges and sockets.
- Violet identifies the rocket core/engine; amber identifies ignition/momentum/progress; cyan and teal identify reserve, warning, environment, recovery, and navigation subsystems without changing the canonical seven-system meaning.
- Finish hierarchy: satin leather → hammered metallic structure → clear-coated enamel → faceted luminous crystals.

## 7. Identity-defining features

Critical:

1. exactly one central vertical rocket;
2. exactly seven independently countable flight-system modules;
3. one-to-one semantic emblems for ignition, momentum, minimum power, warning, environment, recovery, and weekly navigation;
4. seven visible connector sockets with no floating module;
5. one curved Earth/orbit horizon;
6. one separate seven-cell launch-window arc;
7. presentation-only hierarchy that never owns labels, answers, progress, focus, or persistence.

Important:

- faceted violet rocket core and exhaust crystal;
- physically separate rocket fins and engine collar;
- countable hex cells on the environment shield;
- gridded warning dial and looped recovery route;
- off-axis thickness in all bezels, lenses, and sockets.

## 8. Uncertainty and single-image limits

- The rear faces, undercuts, and exact depth of every module are hidden; their shallow-relief depth will be inferred from the visible contact shadows.
- Connector backs are occluded where they meet the rocket; sockets must overlap enough to prevent gaps without claiming unseen construction accuracy.
- The generated reference includes rich surface mottling that production will approximate procedurally at real-time cost; exact pixel matching is not a goal.
- The source view is a three-quarter whole-book render. The cropped relief reference improves admission and component review but still contains perspective convergence.
- The right parchment page, book shell, tabs, and spine already exist in production and are not part of this reconstruction target.
- Exact written labels are deliberately excluded. Canonical Chapter VI words and player values remain in the DOM/state layer.
