# Chapter V Quest Forge — projection route

Decision: **projection is not required**.

The accepted image is a stylized production target rather than a photographed object whose exact printed albedo must be recovered. Its identity is carried by countable closed solids, real bevels, facet topology, sockets, hardware, page contact, independent PBR response, and controlled emissive geometry. Projecting the reference would bake lighting and risk a convincing broadside image over shallow or false structure.

Implementation route: procedural closed geometry plus independently authored materials. Sample palette/value relationships from the reference, use geometry for silhouette-changing relief and repeated hardware, use separate procedural albedo/roughness/normal/AO evidence for important surfaces, and validate with map-stripped and off-axis renders.
