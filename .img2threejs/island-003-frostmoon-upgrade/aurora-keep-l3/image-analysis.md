# Aurora Keep L3 candidate image analysis

## Identification

The candidate is a stylized northern civic keep: a fortified timber-and-stone great hall combining defense, settlement leadership and warm communal habitation. It is a compact compound hard-surface subject. Confidence: 0.98. Primary domain: object/architecture.

## Overall form and silhouette

A raised frost-stone base supports a tall central gable hall. Two dominant round guard towers bracket the front, lower residential wings step down to both sides, and a small gatehouse projects from the centre. Warm-copper roofs remain visible beneath neutral snow. Paired brass ring instruments form the final ridge accent. The keep should read as a coherent settlement headquarters, not a ring of repeated turrets, a blue ice castle, a palace or an alpine lodge.

## Spatial relationships

The great hall is centred on and embedded into the foundation. The gatehouse projects from the +Z face below the watch gallery. The two guard towers occupy the front-left and front-right shoulders and overlap both the base and side-wing masses. The residential wings attach behind the towers and step down from the hall. Copper roofs socket into explicit wall collars; snow sits on those roofs without recolouring them. Ridge rings attach through dark support cradles to the main roof ridge. The rear service wing attaches to the hall's -Z face, with its door, porch, chimney and dressing all contacting the foundation.

## Materials and finish

- Frost stone: dark neutral charcoal, high roughness, broad block courses.
- Dark timber: near-black brown, high roughness, heavy structural thickness.
- Warm copper: brown-orange roof metal, medium roughness, satin weathering and visible seams.
- Cream infill: warm off-white plaster used sparingly between timber frames.
- Brass: warmer and brighter than copper, reserved for rings, trim and door hardware.
- Windows/firelight: cozy amber emissive panes with restrained local light at night.
- Snow/ice: neutral white snow with cool pale blue only in small ice edges; no blue roofs, purple magic or northern lights.
