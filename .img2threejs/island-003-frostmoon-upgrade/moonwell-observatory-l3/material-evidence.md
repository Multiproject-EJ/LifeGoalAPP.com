# Moonwell Observatory L3 material evidence

The user-approved sheet is a neutral stylized concept, not a calibrated material capture. It is therefore treated as palette and construction evidence, while the existing Frostmoon procedural materials remain the runtime authority for relightable day, blizzard, dusk, and night states.

- Frost stone: charcoal and mid-grey procedural stone with high roughness, snow-filled joints, and contact-shadowed courses.
- Dark timber: near-black brown structural cores with object-space grain and lighter rubbed edges.
- Raw copper: warm orange-brown rib cladding and hardware with restrained patina; it must never shift to blue roof metal.
- Brass: brighter warm instrument rings, telescope barrel, spindle, weights, and lantern frames.
- Ice-water: pale cyan translucent basin surface and moon disc; no purple magical crystal remains in the landmark.
- Amber glass: small high-emissive entry lanterns, supported by one quality-gated warm point light for night readability.
- Snow: rough pale caps and joint deposits that remain readable under the island's phase-driven lighting.

Projection is intentionally skipped because it would bake the concept sheet's studio lighting into a landmark that must survive four authored ambience phases.
