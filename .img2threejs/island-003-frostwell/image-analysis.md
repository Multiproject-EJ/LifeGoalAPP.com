# Image analysis — Frostwell Iceworks

The supplied image is an in-engine placement and camera reference for Island
003, not a picture of the proposed machine. It establishes Frostmoon Haven's
locked overview, palette, scale, frozen-ocean field and the protected 36-tile
board. The new asset is therefore an original, contract-led reconstruction.

## Observed evidence

- Frostmoon occupies the centre of a broad pale-cyan frozen ocean.
- Four satellite shelves and the central island already consume the canonical
  landmark plots; none may be displaced.
- The camera is a high three-quarter view. Strong verticals read well, while
  shallow floor detail needs large value/color separation.
- Existing materials are snowy ivory, blue-grey rock, indigo roof, warm window
  light, brass and translucent cyan/violet ice.
- The north ocean provides the strongest locked-camera composition for a
  detached facility while keeping the tile route legible.

## Target decomposition

- Macro: separate cracked sea-ice slab, tall A-frame drill tower, cylindrical
  freshwater tank, low fishery shed, and a slim pier/pipe umbilical.
- Meso: rotating drill head and segmented shaft, winch/flywheel, conveyor loop,
  descending net/bucket carriers, water pipe arches, tank bands and pressure
  gauges.
- Micro: alternating empty/fish-filled carriers, warm lamps, safety rails,
  ice chips, steam/snow construction burst and moving cyan flow slugs.

## Placement and interaction

The root is placed offshore at approximately `[0, -0.3, -9.4]`; its sea-ice
slab remains visibly separated north of the island shelves. A narrow
non-walkable pier and insulated pipe point south toward Frostmoon without
intersecting any board tile. The root and a generous invisible hit target are clickable. Named pivots
own the drill rotation, winch, conveyor carriers and construction burst.

## Confidence and inference

Confidence is high for camera, scale, palette and safe placement. Geometry of
the Iceworks is intentionally inferred from the user's mission description and
the Island 003 art language; the source cannot support pixel-identical claims.
Hidden sides use plausible Nordic-industrial construction and must be reviewed
from left/right orbit views.
