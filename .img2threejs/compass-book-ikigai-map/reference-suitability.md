# Chapter IV Ikigai Map relief — reference suitability

Verdict: **conditional pass** for a stylized, real-time procedural page relief.

- One obvious target assembly is clearly isolated on the left page.
- The complete silhouette, five-node count, pentagon topology, centre hub, materials, and contact frame are visible at useful resolution.
- The shallow hard-surface form can be reconstructed with extruded plates, cylinders, torus/ring solids, curve tubes, shape extrusions, and instanced micro-parts.
- One view hides the reverse and exact contact depths, but the target is a page-bound relief rather than a free-standing mechanical object; closed shallow solids and socket overlaps are a bounded inference.
- Fine chart engraving and pictogram detail may be procedurally simplified, but the five-force graph, separate Trial, candidate routes, and isolated Mirage are strict identity gates.
- No exact logo, label, manufacturing dimension, transparent caustic, smoke volume, or mesh extraction is required.

Required acceptance evidence: fixed high-tier render, low-tier render, two non-degenerate orbit views, map-stripped geometry capture, strict spec validation, runtime part coverage, and comparison against the goal image. The result must be described as an achievable stylized production interpretation, not an exact hidden-side reconstruction.
