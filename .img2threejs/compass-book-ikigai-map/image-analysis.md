# Chapter IV Ikigai Map relief — image analysis

Reference: `docs/gauntlets/evidence/compass-book-inner-goals-2026-08-20/41-wide-chapter-4-goal.png`

## 1. Identification and classification

The target is the **left-page constellation instrument**, not the host book or blank right page. It is a hard-surface, layered, presentation-only game artifact assembled from a contact plate, frame, five perimeter force medallions, a central trial mechanism, connecting rails, candidate-path signals, and a subordinate mirage marker. Primary domain: `object`. Confidence: **0.98**.

## 2. Overall form and silhouette

The footprint is a portrait rectangular inset plate with a large regular pentagonal mechanism centered inside it. Five circular medallions interrupt the pentagon corners; a smaller multi-ring hub occupies the centre. The composition is approximately radial around the hub but intentionally five-fold rather than bilaterally or four-fold symmetric. The relief is shallow relative to its page footprint, with visible stacked bezel, rail, jewel, and plate heights.

## 3. Macro → meso → micro decomposition

- Macro: page contact plate/frame; five-force constellation; central Trial hub; Mirage satellite.
- Meso: five circular node bezels; five icon/jewel inserts; five outer pentagon rails; five hub spokes; three candidate-path arcs; central ring stack and crystal; star-chart field.
- Micro: radial bezel rivets; small socket jewels; chart grooves; sparse star studs; connector collars; broken Mirage tether; enamel facet highlights; shallow material wear/patina.

## 5. Materials and surface

- Contact/chart field: dark navy-black leather/enamel, dielectric, roughness about 0.72, fine paper/leather bump and sparse recessed chart lines.
- Structural rails/bezels/rivets: antique gilt, metallic, roughness about 0.28–0.42 with darker cavity patina.
- Curiosity: violet translucent-looking enamel/crystal with emissive core and clearcoat.
- Capability: teal enamel and pale cyan faceted insert.
- Contribution: rose-gold/copper emblem with satin metal response.
- Viability: amber-gold metallic/enamel insert.
- Willingness: orange-red enamel flame with emissive centre.
- Trial: violet crystal plus gilt rings, brightest emissive anchor.
- Mirage: desaturated charcoal-violet metal/enamel, low emission and higher roughness than valid forces.

## 6. Colour and finish

The host palette remains aged parchment, navy leather, and antique gilt. The five forces are distinguished by violet, teal, rose gold, amber, and ember-orange. The centre repeats violet and gilt but is smaller and structurally inside the graph, so it reads as the chosen experiment rather than a sixth force. Finish alternates between satin metal, glossy enamel, and matte chart field; the goal image's baked highlights are lighting evidence, not albedo.

## 7. Identity-defining features

1. Exactly five countable perimeter force nodes.
2. One uninterrupted five-sided outer rail system and five separate inward spokes.
3. A physically separate central Trial ring/crystal assembly.
4. Distinct nonverbal force inserts: comet, faceted star/tool, contribution beacon/hands, bridge/coin, flame.
5. Three candidate-path arcs with one selected/bright and two subordinate/dim.
6. One dark Mirage marker outside the pentagon with a broken tether only.
7. Dense but subordinate engraved star-chart rings and sparse star/rivet repetitions.
8. Visible bevels, socket collars, page contact, and off-axis relief thickness.

## 8. Uncertainty and single-image limits

The underside, reverse faces, exact socket construction, and hidden portions beneath overlapping rails are not visible. Because the target is a page-bound shallow relief, these can be inferred as closed solids with deliberate overlap rather than invented functional mechanisms. The detailed pictograms are an aspirational ceiling; production may simplify them into clear faceted emblems if count, palette, and semantic separation survive at the normal book camera. Exact text is excluded. Two meaningful orbit captures and a map-stripped render are required before acceptance.

## Production interpretation

The canonical chapter defines five forces—Curiosity, Capability, Contribution, Viability, and Willingness—plus a chosen Trial and possible Mirage warning. The 3D layer may visualize these stable categories and presentation state, but personal labels, answers, candidate-path text, experiment text, warning copy, progress, focus, and persistence remain in the canonical DOM/state layer.
