# Projection route

Verdict: **projection is not required**.

The reference is an ImageGen production ceiling for a relightable, procedural game artifact—not a licensed or identity-critical painted surface whose exact pixels must be preserved. The five-force count, graph topology, node palettes, central Trial, candidate paths, Mirage separation, bevels, and relief depth are the fidelity targets. They will be implemented as real geometry with independent procedural PBR materials.

Photo projection would bake the reference lighting and pictogram shading into albedo, undermine map-stripped proof, and prevent the node emissions and completion animation from responding correctly to the production lights. Procedural canvas textures may supply independent leather/paper bump and roughness evidence only; no generated text or personal answer enters a texture.
