Source: `docs/visual-references/island-018-jungle-expedition/018-source.png`

## 1. Identification And Classification

The image is a tall mobile-game island concept screen showing a floating
jungle lost-city environment around a circular board. For modelling, the target
is the UI-free island environment and landmark family, not the baked interface.
Primary domain: `object`, with architectural, botanical, hard-surface and
waterfall effect systems.

Suitability confidence: 0.82 for a stylized real-time browser reconstruction.
Exact hidden-side fidelity is limited because the source is one front
three-quarter composition.

## 2. Overall Form And Silhouette

The dominant world silhouette is a stacked floating cliff island with a central
stepped cuboid temple mass, asymmetric vertical cliff drops, and smaller
peripheral platforms. The board path forms a circular/elliptical ring in front
of and around the temple. Distant background islands form layered depth behind
the main platform. Multiple waterfalls descend from side cliffs and lower
ledges.

The source perspective is an elevated front three-quarter mobile-game camera.
The north/top of the island recedes away from the viewer, matching the Island
Run visual contract direction.

## 5. Materials And Surface

Observed materials include moss-covered carved stone, wet cliff rock, dense
leaf canopies, rope and aged wood, amber torchlight, gold/brass trim, emerald
gem/glow surfaces, turquoise water and cloud/mist atmosphere. Stone needs
nonuniform albedo, cavity-darkened cracks, edge chips, moss overlays and wet
roughness variation. Vegetation needs varied leaf-card hue/value and layered
alpha-friendly silhouettes.

## 6. Color And Finish

Dominant hues: saturated emerald/olive jungle greens, teal/turquoise water,
warm amber-gold windows and trim, pale limestone blocks, sky blue and white
clouds. Finish contrast matters: satin wet stone, matte moss/leaves, low-gloss
gold trim, emissive green/amber accents and translucent waterfall/mist effects.
