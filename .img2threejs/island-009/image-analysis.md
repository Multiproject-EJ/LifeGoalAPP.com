- basalt/obsidian: charcoal-to-black dielectric stone, high roughness, pitted
  normal relief and emissive crack accents only where heated;
- black steel: near-black metal, medium roughness, edge highlights and sparse

- copper/brass: metallic warm orange/brown or muted gold, medium roughness,
  darker cavity patina and brighter worn edges;
