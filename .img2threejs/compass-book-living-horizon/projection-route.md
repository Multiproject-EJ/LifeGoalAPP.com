# Living Horizon projection route

Decision: **procedural geometry and authored PBR materials; no image projection**.

The admitted image is an ImageGen art-direction target rather than a photograph of a fixed manufactured object. Its painted shadows, highlights, typography, and camera treatment are not safe albedo evidence. Projecting those pixels would make the relief read correctly from only one angle and would undermine the launch requirement for a genuinely dimensional, animated 3D book.

The production model therefore reconstructs the identity-bearing systems as geometry:

- a page-bound double brass frame;
- layered terrain terraces with a real height profile;
- one uninterrupted raised S-path connecting all five zones;
- a sanctuary cottage and teal inlet;
- a workshop roof stack and gear medallion;
- a gathering seating ring and hearth;
- an open gate, amber sun, and separate sun-ray fan.

Materials use solid regional palettes, existing independent book surface maps where appropriate, and physically meaningful roughness/metalness/clearcoat values. Lighting remains scene-owned. The generated goal image is retained for composition, hierarchy, color, and semantic comparison only.
