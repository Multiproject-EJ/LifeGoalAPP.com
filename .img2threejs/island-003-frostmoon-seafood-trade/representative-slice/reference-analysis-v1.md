## Identification

The target is a stylized long-haul winter seafood freight vehicle. Primary domain is `object`; structure is an articulated mechanical assembly. Confidence is 0.98.

## Overall form and silhouette

The vehicle occupies a long, low cuboid deck roughly 2.8 times its visible wheelbase height. Four large radial wheels interrupt the lateral silhouette, while paired long ski runners extend beyond both ends. A vertical mast rises from the forward third and supports one asymmetric triangular sail. The overall construction is bilaterally paired around the longitudinal axis, while cargo placement is intentionally irregular.

Production will use a stylized fixed dual-contact wheel/ski system, not claim manufacturable mechanics. Sail deformation will be a bounded visual animation rather than cloth simulation.
