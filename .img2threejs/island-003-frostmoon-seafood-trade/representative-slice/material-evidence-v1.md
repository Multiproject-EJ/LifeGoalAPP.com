# Freight cart material evidence v1

The concept sheet is secondary-inferred studio artwork, so its pixels are not treated as exact recovered PBR maps. Production uses independent live materials compatible with the existing Frostmoon day, blizzard, dusk and night lighting:

- dark timber: `#3a261e`, metalness 0, roughness 0.84 with directional grain normal;
- hammered copper: `#95502f`, metalness 0.86, roughness 0.38 with dimpled normal and edge/contact variation;
- cream sail cloth: `#e0d5ba`, metalness 0, roughness 0.84, double-sided with woven normal;
- packed ice: `#d5e9ef`, metalness 0, roughness 0.24 with faceted normal;
- seafood: silver-grey fish plus restrained orange crab accents, roughness 0.42–0.62.

Albedo, roughness, normal intent and ambient-occlusion response remain independent. No concept-sheet highlight or shadow is baked into runtime albedo.
