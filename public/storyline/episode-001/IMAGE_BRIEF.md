# Welcome Aboard — image brief

These panels are placeholders until the opening cinematic art is produced.

1. **Ordinary life:** a customizable young player, provisionally presented as a
   boy, leans casually against the Observation Deck glass. The pose sells the
   understatement of “Yeah… it’s not that bad, really.”
2. **Scale reveal:** pull far back to reveal a warm, astonishing diplomatic
   spaceship and the 120-island route. This is wonder, not emergency.
3. **Creation Halls:** an immense “universe in the basement” with fabrication
   rings turning holographic ideas into safe prototypes.
4. **Bot family:** the familiar white-and-blue circular PA in a transparent
   glassworker visor beside varied builder robots with distinct faces and tools.
5. **Mission departure:** Luma Isle ahead, builders ready, no warning lights and
   no visual hint of the Great Drift.

Keep the future humane and lived-in. Medical abundance, slow ageing, and 247
years of peace should be conveyed through calm normality rather than exposition
screens.
