# Creation Halls image brief

1. **Hall reveal:** wide, bright fabrication chamber with circular ceiling
   machinery, holographic blueprints, clean workbenches, and many non-humanoid
   builder robots. Optimistic rather than industrially grim.
2. **PA work helmet:** close view of the existing circular white-and-blue mascot
   with its friendly face fully visible through a transparent glassworker safety
   visor. Helmet reads as removable equipment.
3. **Chief Builder:** stockier robot from the same manufacturing family, amber
   accents, angular eyes, blueprint projector, reinforced arms, and a distinct
   helmet silhouette.
4. **Request console:** player-facing holographic console where an idea becomes a
   blueprint and then a physical prototype. No unrestricted “magic wish” visual.

Do not show weapons, Drift corruption, damage, threat, or human workers.
