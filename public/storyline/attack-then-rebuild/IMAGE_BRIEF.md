# Attack Then Rebuild — image brief

- Target only an automated defense, hostile machine, corrupted Arena, or
  evacuated installation; no graphic harm.
- Reuse the same location before and after the operation so the consequence is
  legible.
- Builder bots exchange tactical equipment for fabrication tools in view of the
  player.
- Military liaison styling should look official and trusted at first. The
  unease comes from missing context, not obvious evil coding.
