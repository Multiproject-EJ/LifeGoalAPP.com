# Creature-first progressions v2 — Fern, Nightbloom, Aurora and Cosmos

Date: 2026-09-20
Status: selected review candidates; not approved art or 3D meshes

## Why this revision exists

The first preferred-family pass overcorrected away from recognisable animals and produced too many living sculptures. The user clarified the roster norm: most designs must read immediately as creatures. Abstract organisms are welcome as contrast, not as the default.

The production rule is now explicit and visible in Art production:

- at least **40 of 50** families must be creature-bodied;
- at most **10 of 50** may be deliberate abstract or living-monument exceptions;
- “creature-bodied” requires locomotion, an attention focus, expressive posture and a coherent animate body;
- this does not permit a real animal with decorative accessories, a generic quadruped template or shared glossy-eye face.

Dreamroot and Prism remain the abstract exceptions in this preferred slice. Fern, Nightbloom, Aurora and Cosmos were rebuilt through every existing form.

The Nightbloom middle-form first revision was rejected because it retained the hatchling crouch. The selected middle lifts the torso, extends the struts, moves the sensory face beyond cover and splits the mantle into two functional fins.
