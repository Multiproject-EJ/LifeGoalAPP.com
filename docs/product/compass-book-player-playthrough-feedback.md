# Compass Book founder playthrough

Status: editorial acceptance loop
Player: Eivind
Purpose: test whether the complete Book produces a useful, recognisable and
actionable reading without forcing identity claims.

## How to play it

Complete the Book as a real player. Do not optimise answers for the design and
do not repair awkward questions in your head. When a prompt is narrow,
confusing or missing your real answer, record that friction before moving on.

After each chapter, capture:

1. **Recognition:** Which output felt genuinely recognisable? Which felt generic
   or false?
2. **Evidence:** Did the Book ask for enough real moments and behaviour before
   naming a pattern?
3. **Possibility space:** Which answer was missing? Did a list make you choose a
   category that was only approximately true?
4. **Friction:** Which interaction was too long, repetitive, vague or
   emotionally demanding?
5. **Inference:** Where did the Book claim more than the answers justified?
6. **Usefulness:** What decision, boundary, experiment, goal or habit became
   clearer because of the chapter?
7. **Surprise:** What did the Book reveal that you had not already formulated?
8. **Revision:** What would make you revisit this reading, and did the Book make
   revision feel legitimate?

## Per-prompt shorthand

Use any of these labels while playing:

- `NARROW` — my real answer was missing;
- `VAGUE` — I could not tell what evidence the prompt wanted;
- `IDEAL` — I answered as the person I admire, not from behaviour;
- `FORCED` — two or more answers were equally true;
- `REPEAT` — the Book asked the same thing without adding depth;
- `HEAVY` — too much effort for the value produced;
- `LEAP` — the output inferred more than I supplied;
- `GOOD` — the question clarified something useful;
- `SURPRISE` — it revealed a pattern I had not named;
- `ACTION` — it led naturally to a boundary, experiment, goal or habit.

## Acceptance standard

A chapter is ready when:

- the player can supply a truthful answer without squeezing into the nearest
  attractive label;
- major outputs can be traced back to visible evidence;
- contradictions and uncertainty remain visible;
- the resulting page is concise enough to use in a decision;
- the next action is a small test or boundary, not a life declaration;
- revisiting later feels like learning rather than admitting failure.
