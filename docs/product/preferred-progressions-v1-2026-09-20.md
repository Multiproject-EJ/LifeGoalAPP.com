## Rendering contract

- Clay images are small, rounded, matte, 4/10-detail modelling references on a neutral studio ground.
- Cinematic images use each selected clay image as the sole topology, palette and pose reference, then translate it into realistic living materials and dramatic light.
- Clay and cinematic assets express the same creature identity; neither asset is a rotatable model.
- Legacy animal nouns do not authorise animal anatomy. The prompts explicitly excluded the associated animal as well as named-franchise likeness.
- “Generated” means candidate, never approval or exhaustive look-alike clearance.

## Rejected directions

- Fern: rejected a taller draft with floating attention marks; selected the low, fully connected root-hook.
- Dreamroot: rejected ordinary mushroom and building/tree-like drafts; selected angular shelter blades, three root ribbons and one suspended core.
- Nightbloom: rejected an upright robed/humanoid final; selected a wide low alcove.
- Prism: rejected a wrapped human/baby-like middle; selected a low lens core. Its first cinematic form-3 image was also rejected for showing three suns and regenerated with one sun and three reflections.
- Aurora: rejected a generic baby-animal face, then a transparency/cropping failure; selected a continuous sideways figure-eight ribbon with no head.

The selected/rejected source IDs and local filenames are recorded in `preferred-progressions-v1-manifest.json`.
