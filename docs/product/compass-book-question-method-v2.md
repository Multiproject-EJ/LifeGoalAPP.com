# Compass Book Question Method v2

Status: product-method proposal for joint review
Priority: Chapter II, The Inner Compass
Scope: philosophical and elicitation quality; no gameplay-state ownership changes

## Decision

Keep the six-chapter arc, the 20-island cadence, the canonical chapter IDs, and
the existing one-page outputs. Change how the Book earns those outputs.

The Book must not claim to discover a fixed true self from a sequence of
attractive labels. It builds a dated, revisable working model from remembered
episodes, repeated behaviour, trade-offs, counterevidence, and small real-world
tests.

The method is:

> Observe -> name a possible pattern -> look for contradiction -> form a
> hypothesis -> test it -> preserve a seasonal reading.

## Promise to the player

The Compass Book does not know the player better than the player and does not
silently turn an answer into identity. It helps the player notice evidence they
might otherwise miss and assemble a useful current reading.

Every major conclusion must be presented as one of:

- an observation;
- an interpretation;
- a working hypothesis;
- a tested finding;
- an unresolved question.

"Sealed" means preserved as historical truth about what the player believed at
that time. It never means permanently true.

## Question-quality contract

1. **Behaviour before labels.** Identity-relevant choices follow a concrete
   episode, action, or repeated pattern.
2. **Contrasts before absolutes.** Ask when a claim is true and when it is not.
3. **Trade-offs before ideals.** Values are evidenced by choices under cost, not
   merely by admirable words.
4. **Context before trait.** Separate current state, recurring pattern,
   preference, aspiration, constraint, and hypothesis.
5. **Uncertainty is valid data.** Important choice pools offer `combination`,
   `something_else`, `depends`, or `not_sure` where appropriate.
6. **No silent inference.** Projected outputs remain proposals until the player
   confirms them.
7. **Experiments before causality.** Engine, lever, fit, calling, and habit
   claims remain hypotheses until real-world evidence supports them.
8. **Counterevidence is invited.** Every chapter has at least one explicit
   contradiction check and one alternative explanation.
9. **Low-friction depth.** Evidence prompts ask for a private phrase, not an
   essay. Most fragments remain tappable; deeper text is concentrated at stage
   boundaries.
10. **Recovery over compliance.** Difficulty, illness, care obligations,
    changing circumstances, and returning after a miss are normal operating
    conditions.

## Shared 20-island method

| Islands | Purpose | Evidence standard |
|---|---|---|
| 1-4 | Observe revealing episodes | At least one concrete moment per major signal |
| 5-8 | Compare repeated patterns and trade-offs | Behaviour plus a cost or contradiction |
| 9-12 | Examine context and constraints | Distinguish temporary state from recurring need |
| 13-16 | Form and challenge interpretations | Trigger, response, consequence, alternative |
| 17-19 | Choose a boundary or small test | Specific if-then action and review trigger |
| 20 | Preserve the seasonal reading | Statement, confidence, unresolved question, confirmation |

## Interaction pattern

The normal fragment should use progressive disclosure:

1. a one-tap memory anchor or short private evidence phrase;
2. a choice that names what the evidence may indicate;
3. an optional `That is not quite it` route;
4. a confidence tap: tentative, plausible, strong;
5. only at important transitions, a counterexample or experiment.

The Book may offer AI help to clarify or compare answers, but AI may only
propose language and patterns. It may not select an answer, raise confidence,
or rewrite a sealed output.

## Chapter II exemplar: The Inner Compass v2

Canonical outputs remain:

- True North;
- Life Spark;
- Essential Need;
- Shadow Pull;
- Guardian Boundary;
- Compass statement.

### Stage 1 — Episodes that reveal direction

#### 21. A moment I felt alive

- Evidence: "Recall one recent moment when you felt unusually alive or
  absorbed. What was happening?" (private phrase, 5-25 words)
- Pattern: "What part of that moment seems most important?" Existing energy
  choices plus combination / something else / not sure.
- Preserve canonical projected ID: `alive_context`.

#### 22. A choice I respect

- Evidence: "Recall a time you respected how you acted, especially if it was
  inconvenient. What did you choose?"
- Pattern: "Which value might that action have expressed?"
- Preserve canonical projected ID: `proud_value`.

#### 23. A moment I lost myself

- Evidence: "Recall a specific moment when you acted unlike the person you
  wanted to be. What pressure or trigger was present?"
- Pattern: "What response took over?"
- Preserve canonical projected ID: `unlike_self`.

#### 24. What I repeatedly move toward

- Evidence: "Across the last year, what experience have you repeatedly made
  time for, returned to, or missed when it disappeared?"
- Pattern: "What might you have been seeking through it?"
- Preserve canonical projected ID: `seeking`.

### Stage 2 — Values evidenced by trade-offs

#### 25. What I protected at a cost

- Evidence: "When did protecting something important cost you approval,
  comfort, time, money, or opportunity?"
- Pattern: "What were you protecting?"
- Preserve canonical projected ID: `protected_value`.

#### 26. Values I claim — and their cost

- Choice: select up to three candidate values.
- Trade-off: "If two of these conflict, which one usually wins in your actual
  behaviour?"
- Counterweight: "Which one sounds admirable but has the least evidence in your
  recent life?"
- Preserve canonical projected ID: `core_values`; add evidence IDs rather than
  replacing the canonical answer.

#### 27. The value visible this week

- Evidence: "What did you spend time, attention, or courage on this week?"
- Pattern: "Which value is most visible in that behaviour?"
- Preserve canonical projected ID: `behavioral_value`.

#### 28. A value missing from my life

- Choice: "Which value matters but has little room in your current season?"
- Contradiction: "Is it missing because circumstances block it, because another
  value currently outranks it, or because it is more aspirational than lived?"
- Preserve canonical projected ID: `missing_value`.

### Stage 3 — Needs revealed by deprivation and restoration

#### 29. What I need to function

- Evidence anchor: "Think of a period when you functioned steadily. What was
  reliably present?"
- Preserve canonical ID: `foundational_needs`.

#### 30. What helps me grow

- Evidence anchor: "Think of a period of genuine growth. What condition helped
  without destabilising the rest of your life?"
- Preserve canonical ID: `growth_needs`.

#### 31. The absence that shows first

- Evidence: "When your functioning begins to deteriorate, what is usually
  missing first? What is the earliest observable sign?"
- Preserve canonical ID: `neglected_need`; add an `early_need_signal` phrase.

#### 32. The need worth designing around

- Choice: "Which need repeatedly proves costly to ignore?"
- Alternative: "Could this be a temporary recovery need rather than a lasting
  requirement?"
- Confidence: tentative / plausible / strong.
- Preserve canonical projected ID: `essential_need`.

### Stage 4 — Strength, overuse, and shadow as a sequence

#### 33. What others repeatedly rely on

- Evidence: "Name two situations in which someone relied on you. What did you
  actually do?"
- Pattern: "Which strength might connect those situations?"
- Preserve canonical projected ID: `strength`.

#### 34. When the strength overshoots

- Sequence: "When that strength is under pressure, what does too much of it look
  like? What short-term problem does it solve?"
- Preserve canonical ID: `overuse`.

#### 35. My recurring drift loop

- Sequence: trigger -> automatic response -> short-term relief -> later cost.
- Pattern choice follows the sequence rather than preceding it.
- Preserve canonical projected ID: `shadow`.

#### 36. A counterbalance with evidence

- Prompt: "What has actually helped interrupt this pattern before?"
- Choice: name the most plausible counterbalance.
- Preserve canonical projected ID: `counterbalance`.

### Stage 5 — Alignment, boundary, and seasonal reading

#### 37. How alignment appears in behaviour

- Evidence: "During a reasonably aligned week, what would another person notice
  you doing differently?"
- Preserve canonical ID: `alignment_signals`.

#### 38. The situation that pulls me off course

- Evidence: "Recall the last occurrence. What happened immediately before the
  drift?"
- Choice: likely cause, with `combination` and `not_sure` available.
- Preserve canonical projected ID: `drift_cause`.

#### 39. A boundary as a testable rule

- Replace a vague aspiration with: "When [recurring situation], I will [specific
  protective action], because it protects [value or need]."
- Add: "What is most likely to make this boundary fail?"
- Preserve canonical projected ID: `guardian_boundary`.

#### 40. Set the current compass

- Compass statement: one sentence combining direction, need, drift, and return.
- Confidence: tentative / plausible / strong.
- Counterevidence: "What does not yet fit this reading?" (optional but visible).
- Review trigger: date or meaningful change in work, health, relationship, home,
  responsibility, or season.
- Confirmation copy: "This is my best current reading. Preserve it in the Inner
  Compass."
- Preserve canonical projected ID: `compass_statement`.

## Chapter III implementation: The Living Horizon v2

The Horizon is no longer assembled primarily from imagined lifestyle labels.
Each major choice now follows a lived ordinary-day clue, contrast or trade-off.
Place, relationships, work, responsibility, money and time are treated as a
system with constraints rather than independent wishes.

The final output is a current life-design hypothesis with evidence count,
confidence, counterevidence and a review trigger. Existing canonical question
IDs and sealed v1 snapshots remain readable.

## Chapter IV implementation: The Ikigai Map v2

The opening Curiosity stage now uses a funnel:

1. select every genuinely recurring interest from a 32-domain inventory;
2. rank the selected set by relative preference;
3. rank the same set by actual discretionary time;
4. consciously lock 3–5 current exploration priorities;
5. use those priorities as the option pool for the Spark hypothesis.

Later stages ground Gift in demonstrated and growing capability, Contribution
in contact with real people and problems, Viability in observable signals, and
Willingness in experienced process and beginner costs. The chapter ends with a
falsifiable small trial. `Trial ready` means a test has been specified; it does
not mean the direction has been proven or constitutes a calling.

## Output-language changes

Avoid fixed-identity language in projections and the Reading page:

- `Your true north` -> `Your current True North reading`
- `Your natural strength` -> `A strength with current evidence`
- `Your shadow` -> `A recurring drift pattern you noticed`
- `This reflects what guides me` -> `This is my best current reading`

The display should distinguish:

- evidence count;
- confidence;
- unresolved contradiction;
- last reviewed date;
- whether a claim has been tested outside the Book.

## Backward-compatible rollout requirements

The current implementation has one global `COMPASS_CURRICULUM_VERSION = 'v1'`,
and book fetches filter on that value. Do not simply bump it while shipping a
Chapter II rewrite; that could make existing books appear absent.

Required migration design:

1. Add chapter-specific method/content versions while retaining the global book
   curriculum identity.
2. Keep existing canonical question IDs when their meaning remains compatible.
3. Add evidence, confidence, contradiction, and experiment answers under new
   IDs; never overwrite old answer records.
4. Keep sealed v1 Chapter II snapshots readable and historically complete.
5. Offer sealed v1 players an explicit "Revisit with the evidence method"
   action. Never unseal automatically.
6. For in-progress v1 chapters, retain completed fragments and introduce new
   evidence blocks as optional supplements until the next explicit chapter
   review. Do not take progress away.
7. Update projections additively so old answers still produce the existing
   canonical outputs and new answers can attach confidence/evidence metadata.
8. Test local and Supabase serialization, old-row loading, completion, Reading
   output, Island fragment answering, AI hints, and the personality shadow
   bridge before enabling v2 by default.

## Admission gate for the live rewrite

Chapter II v2 is ready to implement only when:

- the 20 prompts pass the question-quality contract;
- no canonical output depends on an unsupported inference;
- the low-friction route can be completed without essays;
- `other`, `combination`, and uncertainty have defined behavior;
- existing v1 progress and sealed outputs remain visible;
- AI hints remain suggestions and cannot answer for the player;
- the Reading page labels outputs as current, dated, and revisable.
