## Art acceptance contract

### Progression correction — little → middle → powerful actualisation

The user rejected the near-identical Twilight forms. Each growth line now requires a meaningful anatomical transformation, not just larger size, new eye direction, extra glow or ornament. Form 1 shows little/protected potential; form 2 develops a distinctive usable anatomy and emerging ability; the final form expresses that family's personality with presence, command and fully realised anatomy. Power need not mean aggression.

Approval gate: stages must remain distinguishable as equal-height black silhouettes, without colour, labels, effects or apparent image scale. Change at least two structural dimensions at each transition (body organisation, proportions, stance/contact pattern, negative space, deployment of family-specific anatomy). Retain three family landmarks. Mature facial emotion must remain readable and cannot default to baby eyes or angry eyebrows.

Do not repeat Twilight's root-limb/canopy solution across the roster. Shelter families may develop enclosing strength; resonant families may unfold acoustic structures; exploratory families may deploy sensory anatomy. These are direction examples, not instructions to redesign all families into one template. Record each family's own transformation mechanism before producing its line.

Twilight v2 is the first revised study, not an approved universal solution; see `twilight-evolution-v2-2026-09-19.md`. The middle face and final canopy associations still need review. This changes the art contract, not stored form counts, unlock rules, personality scores or rewards.

Next contrast slice is now reviewable: Echo forms 1–3 and Bloom's existing forms 1–2, both cinematic/clay. Echo uses aperture/resonator expansion; Bloom uses protective body folds and sheltering reach. See `echo-bloom-evolution-v1-2026-09-19.md`. Do not clone either mechanism across the roster. Bloom's possible third stage remains a catalogue/migration decision, not an implicit art-side unlock.

The next preferred-family slice is also reviewable: Cinder retains two forms and changes from a sealed ember-seed to a load-bearing shelter arch; Celest retains two forms and changes from a hooded core with a tethered shard to a broken upright frame; Lux retains three forms and changes from a compact keel through an articulated body to a monumental three-arch organism. - Shared identity does not force shared rendering. Clay figures share a simplified finish; cinematic cards translate each material brief into convincing living surfaces and dramatic light. Neither requires identical bodies, round heads, eye spacing or smiles.
- Two families must differ visibly on at least four identity dimensions: silhouette, body organisation, attention anatomy, material, palette placement and motion. This is a review rubric, not an automated guarantee of uniqueness.
- Form-1 softness is optional, not compulsory. Echo can be eyeless even as a young form. Forms 2+ must not reuse a generic glossy paired-eye face.
- An actual animal with decorative additions fails. No mouse/fox/cat/otter/bird anatomy simply because the legacy name contains that word.
- Test face crops without labels: dominant emotion must be readable. Then test grayscale silhouettes without names. Reject recolour-only differentiation and mature forms that are only larger, angrier or more armoured.
- A growth line preserves identity while changing posture, material organisation and emotional control. The same family may keep its attention anatomy across forms; the uniqueness requirement is between families, not a demand to replace its face each level.
- Hearth Hare / Sunring Staglet share motives and require explicit differentiation: low warm shelter versus open suspended protective structure. No automatic merge.
- Record source, prompt, candidate version, review notes and approval status for every admitted image. Do not claim raster uniqueness from unique text strings alone.

## Matching statistics contract

### Added interaction pilot — same-pose card pop-out

User requested a tiny rounded 4/10 figure, then clicking the image to reveal the same pose in 3D. Bloom form 2 now has a separate 3D study edition. Its poster comes from the actual model; live reveal uses identical camera/lighting/pose, measured image origin, rotation/reset and accessible return. Original illustrations stay unchanged. This pilot is not another finished family or approved form. Review the interaction before expanding per-family models; never apply the same generic body across the roster. See `creature-card-popout-2026-09-19.md`.

The player can inspect best overall and best owned matches, scored family count, their selected team's breadth and close alternatives. Affinity is a 0–100 game overlap index, not a probability or percent of their identity. Complement is additional represented breadth after the current Kindred and Favourite; it is not the least-similar creature by definition. An already selected anchor is excluded from Complement recommendations; unknown or incomplete profiles remain unavailable. Sorting cannot equip, grant, sell or mutate anything.

Keep foundation and direct-motivation scores separate. Ranking is global within the currently selected model; filters do not silently change the rank. Tied rounded scores share a displayed rank. Show unowned matches as discoverable, not available to equip.
