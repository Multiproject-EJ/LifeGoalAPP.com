## Mission and observable outcome

Build Island 014 as a unique, interactive actual-3D Honeycomb Kingdom: a
bright floating bee civilization of amber honey-rock, hexagonal terraces,
flower gardens, honey falls, airborne workers and a dominant royal hive
palace. The real 36-tile board, five canonical landmarks, token, Build flow,
camera, rewards and gameplay authority remain shared and unchanged.

Every landmark must use the existing three-robot construction theatre across
L0→L1, L1→L2 and L2→L3. Each funded level finishes with a short, satisfying
presentation-only commissioning beat: the newly revealed additive geometry
overshoots scale slightly and settles into place while warm light flashes and
small honey-gold sparkles radiate from authored contact points. The effect must
read as a build "pop", not as whole-building teleportation or camera shake.

## Non-negotiables and forbidden shortcuts

- Runtime/file number 014 wins over the baked `ISLAND 024` label.
- Preserve the source's bee-civilization identity, gold/amber/cream palette,
  hexagonal architecture, honey falls, flowers, floating depth and dominant
  central hive palace.
- Keep the real `spark36_ring`; never bake source tiles, HUD, text, counters,
  token or buttons into world geometry.
- Island 014 is ordinary, not an every-fifth Arena island. The source's Arena
  idea becomes the canonical Mystery landmark presentation; the central site
  remains the Boss landmark.
- Author five distinct footprint-stable landmark families with explicit L1–L3
  additive growth and compact honey-language construction rigs.
- All three robots participate at every landmark. Construction presentation
  may read canonical state but never write gameplay.
- The finish pop affects additive/recently commissioned geometry only. It may
  not move hit targets, funded geometry, the board, token, camera or gameplay
  state, and it must be reduced-motion safe.
- Sparkles and flashes must be brief, deterministic, pooled/low-cost and
  non-strobing; reduced motion substitutes a restrained glow pulse.
- Preserve unrelated user work and the untracked robot-family evidence.

## Mission playability and reservoir climax — 2026-08-27

### Observable player outcome

The Great Honeyfall is a complete four-beat mission rather than a hidden state
mechanic. A player can discover the mission through the shared expedition
phone or by tapping the palace reservoir, recognize the four glowing royal
nectar pickups, see each pickup acknowledged, explicitly pour it into the
palace, and watch one persistent central reservoir fill in real time. The
fourth pour builds pressure, bulges and breaks a wax seal, releases the Great
Honeyfall across the cliff, and only then presents mission completion.

### Non-negotiables

- Canonical roll/action services remain the only writers of nectar inventory,
  activated stages and completion time.
- React owns only modal/queue/camera presentation. The Three.js world consumes
  committed state and never writes gameplay.
- A royal-nectar pickup queues its mission presentation behind any Concord or
  other full-attention pickup instead of being silently lost or stacked.
- The shared mission phone shows `0–4` reservoir stages, whether nectar is
  ready, and one clear pour/replay action. No legacy in-world pill is restored.
- The palace reservoir exposes a real 3D hit target. Tapping it opens mission
  control; it never spends nectar by surprise.
- Stages 1–3 raise the same large reservoir liquid line and keep earlier work
  visible. Stage 4 adds the larger pressure/wax-burst/cascade payoff.
- Honey Egg Comb couriers remain an intentional bee-brood civilization motif
  and visibly orbit/work around the reservoir.
- The completion surface waits until the unobstructed 3D climax has played.
- Reduced motion removes oscillation, moving particles and seal debris while
  retaining the full reservoir and completed Honeyfall.
