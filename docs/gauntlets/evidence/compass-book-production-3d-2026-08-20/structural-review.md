# Compass Book structural-pass review

## Decision

`continue` at estimated fidelity **0.77**. The current pass clears the 0.70 global threshold and the only structural critical target, `compass-cover-assembly`, scores 0.82 against its 0.80 minimum.

## What the pass proves

- The cover, spine/binding, layered compass relief, border rails/corners, clasp, and grouped fore-edge tabs are distinct structural systems rather than a fused visual card.
- Left and right orbit captures retain thickness, attachment, and relief; the book does not collapse into a flat plane.
- Repeated hardware and navigation parts remain aligned from both sides.

## Remaining mismatch

- The reference is taller and exposes a deeper parchment page block; the current evidence camera is more top-down and crops that thickness cue.
- Corner plates, binding bands, compass rays, and clasp ornament are simpler than the concept.
- The current brass and indigo response is darker and less nuanced. That is deliberately judged again in the material, surface, and lighting passes.
- Tier-1 silhouette figures are non-authoritative because both dark-background masks fell back to nearly the whole frame. The pass decision comes from direct comparison and three-angle inspection, not the reported 0.9942 IoU.
