# Island 020 — actual 3D rebuild Gauntlet

Status: approved for representative-slice production by Eivind on 2026-08-30

## Mission

Replace the rejected flat/generic visual families with a genuine volcanic island that works as an environment: closed terrain volume, coherent 360° buildings, a functional multi-level orthogonal Level-3 labyrinth, contained flowing lava and a physically clear Iron Skiff escape from summit to Expedition Ship.

## Why the process changed

The source-led hybrid reproduced colour and composition but delegated the island itself to one camera-registered plate. The earlier procedural family supplied orbitable geometry but read as a generic flat disc made from repeated primitives. Both routes failed the user's closer visual inspection. This rebuild starts from a blank actual-3D macro family and forbids both shortcuts.

## Non-negotiables

- No runtime environment plate, billboard island, camera-dependent depth or one-sided building.
- Preserve the real 36-tile route, token, rewards, stops, Arena cadence, build funding and canonical state/actions.
- Real terrain has a closed underside and coherent rear; every stair, doorway, wall and channel terminates physically.
- Lava is contained by geometry, moves downhill, falls through real cliff cuts and illuminates adjacent surfaces without flattening them.
- The labyrinth has one connected 9×9 orthogonal navigation field, three readable junction decisions and one collision-free summit-to-front skiff route; concentric rails are explicitly forbidden because they were the dominant failed-family cue.
- All five landmarks retain coherent additive L1–L3 growth and 15 authored construction transitions.

## Representative vertical slice

The first slice contains only naked macro geometry for the continuous terrain/cliff shell, recessed lava network, buried citadel foundation, central keep, connected orthogonal maze, three gatehouses and summit launch/escape opening. It deliberately excludes final materials, particles, inhabitants, detailed landmarks and animation polish.

Required evidence: canonical phone, left/right/rear, top clearance, low front cliff, clay, semantic ID and wireframe. Every required silhouette/volume/assembly score must reach 0.85. The least flattering orbit governs. A camera-dependent plane, open rear, flat disc, random-box maze or non-functional path retires the family immediately.

## Milestones

1. Admission: lock baseline, 32-part hierarchy, boundaries and representative slice; obtain approval.
2. Macro proof: build and review naked actual-3D terrain + Level-3 labyrinth slice.
3. World systems: contained lava, stairs, bridges, horizon and material/light hierarchy.
4. Landmarks: one coherent family at a time, L1→L3, then construction choreography.
5. Mission: skiff assembly, physical swept-clearance route, ship/cradle and controller presentation.
6. Life/optimization: caretaker, inhabitants, ambience, LOD/batching/culling.
7. Final: source comparison, 360 evidence, phone QA, reduced motion, tests, budgets and physical-device evidence.

## Budgets and stop conditions

High target: at most 175 draw calls, 180k triangles, 50 FPS average, p95 at most 29 ms and slow frames at most 15%. One naked blockout plus one bounded correction per construction family. Retire a family on a hard-veto or a critical score below 0.50; allow one correction only when the defect hypothesis is falsifiable and the worst dimension can improve at least 0.10. Do not add detail around failed macro form.

## Authority boundary

Repository-local reversible code, tests, reference packets and evidence are authorized after admission approval. No merge, deployment, publication, paid/neural service, manual-DCC switch, deletion of historical evidence or phone installation is authorized without a separate instruction.

## Family v4 correction decision — 2026-08-30

Family v4 replaced the decorative concentric rails with a deterministic connected 9×9 orthogonal maze, removed the luminous outer racetrack, narrowed the keep, introduced irregular catchbasins and flat recessed lava feeds, and shifted the light rig toward localized furnace heat. All 1,919 Island Run tests pass. The post-correction phone evidence still scores 0.58 in its weakest critical dimension: the maze reads as a dark stepped block mass, lava is a pale foreground ribbon instead of a convincing illuminated network, and the live scene reports 238 calls / 247k triangles. Under the one-blockout-plus-one-correction rule, this construction family is retired rather than receiving cosmetic detail. A third family or imported/manual-DCC representation requires new authority.
