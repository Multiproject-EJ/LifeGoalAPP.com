# Island 006 Moonveil Neon 3D World Gauntlet

## Mission

Author the sixth real-time procedural Island Run world as a dark, luminous celestial island inspired by the user-supplied reference. It must feel markedly different from Islands 001-005 while preserving the exact live board, camera, landmark, movement, build-mode, and phone-performance contracts.

This island is the next implementation priority. The queued Compass Book 3D micro-detail pass resumes immediately after this island clears its visual and technical gates.

## Authority order

1. The user-supplied dark neon celestial world image is the visual identity authority.
2. The live 36-tile route, five landmark anchors, phone camera kit, and gameplay architecture contracts are immutable.
3. Existing Island 006 canon is retained where compatible: Noctyra, the Moon Gate, lunar/astral motifs, indigo stone, violet crystal, and gold trim.
4. The older tropical Island 006 background is superseded for this 3D world. Its flat artwork may inform landmark function only; it is not a scene, palette, or geometry target.

## Working identity

- Runtime island: 006.
- Authored world source: 006.
- Working world name: **Moonveil Nexus**.
- Role: ordinary island, not an arena island.
- Center landmark: **Noctyra's Moon Gate**, a major buildable astral gateway rather than a creature battle arena.
- Visual thesis: a deep-indigo floating rock constellation, lit by cyan/violet astral energy and restrained warm gold architecture beneath a moving galaxy sky.

## Immutable gameplay geometry

- Keep all 36 code-owned radial tiles and the canonical ring transforms.
- Keep the route center, four outer landmark anchors, bridge approaches, click targets, labels, token motion, tile impacts, and camera presets unchanged.
- Do not bake duplicate tiles, route circles, UI, or interaction affordances into scene art.
- Keep L1-L3 on the same anchor and entrance vector; growth is structural, not only scale.
- Island 006 remains ordinary. The fifth-island arena cadence stays on Island 005.

## World construction contract

### Macro assemblies

1. A broad central floating shelf with a fractured tapered rock root.
2. Four non-identical satellite shelves connected by dark stone-and-gold bridges.
3. A void/galaxy environment with layered star fields and distant floating shards.
4. Cyan-violet energy falls, crystal veins, and under-island glows that establish the neon language.
5. Five authored landmark families with three meaningful build levels each.

### Landmark families

| Anchor | L1 identity | L2 growth | L3 masterwork |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hatchery | Moon-nest pavilion and one luminous egg | enclosed crescent nursery, two nests, working lens | domed celestial nursery, multiple nests, orrery and full crystal lighting |
| Habit | open constellation training court | roofed astral gym, target rig and planetary apparatus | observatory-training complex with canopy, orbit rings and detailed equipment |

### Material hierarchy

- Primary: near-black indigo basalt/astral stone, roughness 0.72-0.92.
- Secondary: satin midnight-blue architectural panels, roughness 0.34-0.55.
- Structural trim: aged warm gold, metalness 0.72-0.9, roughness 0.22-0.38.
- Energy: cyan and violet emissive crystal/portal surfaces with controlled transparency.
- Organic accent: sparse deep teal moss and dark foliage; no bright tropical blanket.
- Warm counterpoint: amber lanterns and windows, limited to architecture and focal navigation.

## Living ambience contract

- Star field uses multiple depth layers so orbiting the camera does not read as a flat wall.
- A slow galaxy/violet nebula rotates independently from the nearer stars.
- Energy waterfalls visibly descend from the shelves and pulse without appearing to enter a water plane.
- Distant shards vary in scale, silhouette, height, rotation, and decoration.
- Sparse meteors, orbiting motes, crescent satellites, and beacon pulses provide low-frequency life.
- Reduced-motion mode freezes decorative drift while keeping camera, interaction, and view culling functional.
- Low quality reduces counts and tessellation, never the main silhouette, route readability, or landmark identity.

## Reference limitations

The supplied image is a single final-angle concept. It is excellent for silhouette, palette, layout language, and lighting but does not reveal hidden rear faces or exact scale. Hidden sides are therefore conditional and must be reconstructed as coherent 360-degree continuations of each visible assembly. No claim of exact mesh extraction is allowed.

## Quality gates

1. Island 006 routes explicitly to authored world source 006; unauthored Island 007 still falls back.
2. The first phone frame reads as dark-neon celestial, not tropical, snowy, bright-cloud, or a recolored Island 005.
3. All 36 top surfaces remain visible with no overlap flicker or missing face.
4. L1/L2/L3 are distinguishable for all five landmarks at 390 x 844 without labels.
5. Front, rear, left, and right orbit views show no holes, floating trim, impossible attachments, or one-sided facades.
6. High mode contains the complete ambience hierarchy. Low mode is materially simpler and remains smooth/readable.
7. The center landmark is impressive but does not occlude the route or masquerade as the Island 005 arena.
8. No gameplay writes are introduced in the renderer.
9. Target quality: at least 8.5/10 whole-world phone view and 8.5/10 landmark focus before approval.

## Failure/stop conditions

- Stop and refine if the scene is merely purple lighting on an existing island.
- Stop and refine if cyan/violet emission washes out gold/stone form separation.
- Stop and refine if the central gate or outer landmarks cross the protected tile corridor.
- Stop and refine if low quality removes semantic objects rather than repeated detail.
- Stop and refine if the user reference and actual phone frame no longer share the same silhouette hierarchy or nocturnal value structure.
