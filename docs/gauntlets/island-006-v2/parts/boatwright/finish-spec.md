# Boatwright finish, after macro approval

Keep approved house, quay, gantry and hull outlines. Reference is a crafted maritime workshop, with visible construction across every face, not a decorated blank cube.

1. Front arched workshop door, recessed dark jamb and chunky timber surround, leaded attic window. Rear access door and attic window, two side windows, sill and diagonal oak braces. Every wall gets structural logic.
2. Curved slate courses follow the one roof surface function. Restrained tile variation; solid ridge/fascia; chimney joints and cap.
3. Quay face: individually chamfered stones with mortar depth; paved top where walking; boat yard timber boards; bollards connected to ground. No wafer-stack base.
4. Hull: curved planking seams, structural ribs inside, sheer rails and transom/stem continuity. Cradle attachments retain burial. Avoid filled box/deck hiding the open boat.
5. Gantry: iron bands and pegs, modest pulley, hanging line and hook, no oversized blank black beams.
6. Final L3 working details: window shutters, workbench/tools, rope coils, barrel, timber stock. Sparse and intentional, with no new gameplay actors.

Budget: replace expensive rounded-box subdivision with chamfered cuboids if required, retaining same dimensions and silhouettes. Compact materials per construction group. Performance measurements precede final handoff.
