# Hatchery: Blender construction family 2, blockout b03

Status: draft macro geometry, awaiting the root's raw browser captures and independent Quality Lord gate. This is the second and final family in the authorized Blender route. The b01/b02 source snapshots and binaries remain unchanged.

## Fundamental representation change

The retired family used Boolean-perforated wall rings and lens-shaped petal lofts. This family creates nursery walls from positive solid piers plus individually extruded arch spandrels. Roofs stand directly on these complete walls. There are no Boolean modifiers or subtractive wall cutters. Glazing is authored under pointed architectural arch boundaries with broad bottom edges on the court; it no longer collapses to a lower leaf point.

The falsifiable hypothesis is that complete occupied nursery rooms and a broad rooted pointed conservatory will remove the floating upper roof and inflated-leaf readings across front, profile, rear and orbit views. It must be assessed from opaque clay and normals as well as transparent beauty. The prior failure is not treated as a material problem.

## Source observations and inferences

The exact approved crop (`intake/hatchery-approved-crop.png`) shows a broad low arched nursery, occupied higher right shoulder, roof court, two sweeping stairs, descending pool landings, one oval egg, and layered pointed glazing on substantial rooted frames. The original crop remains authority. The secondary inferred conservatory clay study was used only to clarify broad lower glazing and arch framing; its camera and inferred geometry were not adopted as approval evidence.

Hidden rear arch spacing, seven-bay plan spacing, wall thicknesses and under-pool gallery structure are authoring inferences. The frozen asset must be assessed for likeness, rather than assuming those inferences are correct.

## Construction and controls

- Main room: continuous interior floor at approximately 0.23, complete perimeter and transverse arcade, broad occupied roof court at 0.995.
- Higher right shoulder: complete additive level-2 arched room from 0.995 to its supported roof at 1.66. Every facade retains solid piers and upper spandrels.
- Conservatory: seven broad rooted glazing bays, tiered pointed tops, continuous root-to-apex main frames, and an explicit front entrance arch. Glazing is paired into a thin closed surface for opaque clay replacement.
- The successful swept stair support method remains, with 22 physically supported treads per side, ending on the unchanged 0.995 court datum. Pool landings now share explicit positive arcade support geometry.
- The single smoothly oval egg and low cradle are retained. No foliage, furniture, finishing textures or lighting changes are used.
- Origin, Y-up/front+Z axes, ground zero, 1.6 footprint limit, canonical lift/egg socket coordinates, owner groups, construction stages, funded-level filtering and root camera controls remain fixed.
- Levels are additive: main nursery, stairs, pools, egg and three conservatory bays are level 1. The upper occupied shoulder and four additional glazing bays are level 2. Level 3 macro equals level 2 until an approved finishing pass.

## Mechanical evidence and reproduction

The source exports three roof-support contracts. Each perimeter/cross-gallery station casts against its actual positive pier mesh; the pier must intersect the roof underside and reach the supporting floor. Each spandrel must have nonzero exported geometry. Checks run before consolidation, and the GLB verifier confirms all corresponding source parts survive export.

`family02-blockout-b03-support-checks.json` contains actual ray results. `family02-blockout-b03-export-checks.json` reads the binary GLB and verifies finite attributes, bounds, indices, owner parents, extras, additive mesh signatures and support-part preservation. These are mechanical checks, not visual approval.

Reproduce from the repository root:

```sh
/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender --background --python scripts/island001-v2-blender/hatchery.py
python3 docs/gauntlets/island-001-v2/blender-route/hatchery/verify-family02-b03.py
```

The frozen `hatchery-family02-b03.py` snapshot is the exact authoring source used for the `.blend` and `.glb` files. Runtime code and public asset locations are outside this worker's ownership. The builder does not run browser review or self-approve.
