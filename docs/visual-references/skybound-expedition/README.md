# Skybound Expedition visual goals

These images are **directional concept targets**, not production assets and not
literal UI specifications. They establish the intended emotional and visual
quality while the shipping game remains original, performant, readable, and
code-native.

## Goal frames

- `skybound-flight-goal-v1.png` — target for the peak flight moment: readable
  forward motion, a cyan wind ring, a curved salvage trail, one obvious hazard,
  modular plane identity, distant Sky Gate, and sparse phone controls.
- `skybound-hangar-goal-v1.png` — target for progression: the same craft must
  visibly change when Launch Rig, Airframe, and Pulse Drive improve.
- `skybound-pilot-academy-flight-goal-v2.png` — approved Pilot Academy gameplay
  destination: Cadet toy glider, slingshot, academy course, five-rank rail,
  Boost, Stabilizer, and one-ticket-per-sortie language.
- `skybound-pilot-academy-evolution-goal-v2.png` — approved five-aircraft
  evolution destination from toy glider to an original non-weaponized fighter,
  plus fictional Gold Wings medal and Skybound pilot certificate.

The v2 Academy frames supersede the generic v1 framing for new design choices.
The v1 images remain as historical evidence for the flight and hangar studies.

## Translation rules

- Preserve the current deterministic 2D simulation and phone-first input.
- Translate the depth, palette, silhouettes, and feedback language; do not use
  the goal images as runtime backgrounds or paint over gameplay readability.
- Every collectible and hazard is simulation-owned, not decorative-only.
- Plane evolution must be visible in the live renderer and must correspond to
  the upgrade that changed the physics.
- Keep the experience humane: no paid pressure, fake scarcity, loot boxes, or
  grind walls.
