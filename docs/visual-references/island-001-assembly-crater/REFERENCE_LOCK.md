  route without crossing its protected inner edge.

  shallow soil collar is stone.

  in the centre of Island 001.

## Blast language

- Every detonation is a readable 2.4-second impact: flash, two expanding pressure
  rings, dust, sparks, arcing stone fragments and temporary camera shake.
- Every fifth detonation is a stronger milestone; the twentieth is the largest.
- Reduced-motion mode reveals the committed sector immediately without shake or
  delayed presentation.
