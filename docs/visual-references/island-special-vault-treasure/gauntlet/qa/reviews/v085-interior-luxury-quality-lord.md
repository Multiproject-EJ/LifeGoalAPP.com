The retained rosettes, jeweled newels, descent halos, darker museum lighting, and framed relic alcoves deliver the intended richness without damaging the staircase silhouette.

## Residual note

The atrium intentionally keeps a bright marble value range to preserve the airy palace character. Future polish should add localized authored textures or baked detail rather than globally darkening the room or adding narrow repeating stair ornaments.
